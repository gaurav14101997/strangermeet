How StrangerMeet Works

StrangerMeet (strangermeet.in) is a fast, browser-based 1-on-1 social discovery and communication platform built for genuine, respectful conversations. Unlike traditional social networks that require lengthy sign-up forms, email verification, or phone numbers, StrangerMeet connects you with another online user in seconds while keeping your identity private until you choose to connect as friends.

Safety & Community Guidelines

Keeping StrangerMeet welcoming and secure is our highest priority. Every user must follow these core community standards:

  • 18+ Adults Only StrangerMeet is strictly intended for users aged 18 years and older. Minors are not permitted to use this service.
  • Zero Tolerance for Abuse Harassment, hate speech, nudity, sexually explicit images, threats, and commercial spam result in immediate disconnection and reporting.
  • Mutual Consent for Calls Voice and video calling features remain locked during random stranger chats and only unlock after both users mutually accept a friend request.
  • Protect Personal Information Never share sensitive personal details such as home addresses, passwords, financial information, or government IDs with strangers online.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is StrangerMeet completely free to use? ▼

Yes. Random 1-on-1 text chat, photo sharing, adding friends, and peer-to-peer WebRTC voice and video calls on strangermeet.in are 100% free with no hidden subscriptions or coin packages.

Do I need to create an account or give my phone number? ▼

No registration, email, or phone number is required. StrangerMeet assigns a secure anonymous session token to your browser so you can start chatting immediately while keeping your saved friends list intact on your device.

How do Voice and Video Calls work? ▼

During a random chat, click Add Friend in the chat header. Once the other user clicks Accept, Phone and Video icons unlock automatically. Calls use encrypted peer-to-peer WebRTC directly between both browsers.

Can I remove or unfriend someone later? ▼

Yes. Open the Friends menu at the top or open your direct friend chat and click the Unfriend icon. Removing a friend immediately revokes direct calling permissions for both sides.

How do I report inappropriate behavior? ▼

Click the flag (Report) icon in the top-right corner of the chat window or press Next to leave immediately. For serious safety concerns, contact us directly via our Contact Us page.
